# Myzone

Reviewed: 2026-10-22. Category: Effort recognition/club engagement. Public-source desk research; no account walkthrough.

## Documented Offer
Personal heart-rate zones and effort points, live feedback, plus in-club/community/coaching offerings.

## What To Learn - Analysis
Recognizes relative effort rather than treating absolute output as universal ability.

## Limits And Questions
Heart-rate-derived effort is not the same as strength skill or safe training progression; broader outcome superiority is untested.

## KRUXT Response - Proposed
Separate adherence, personal improvement and performance; do not invent one universal fitness score.
