# Freeletics

Reviewed: 2026-10-22. Category: Guided training/habits. Public-source desk research; no account walkthrough.

## Documented Offer
Personalized training across several modalities; adjustable workout constraints and challenges.

## What To Learn - Analysis

## Limits And Questions
Its documented weekly streak can break when the originally planned session count is missed; harm/motivation effects are untested here.

## KRUXT Response - Proposed
Offer compassionate restart and planned-frequency recognition rather than compulsory daily exertion.
