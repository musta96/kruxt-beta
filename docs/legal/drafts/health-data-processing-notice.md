## 2. What Can Reveal Health

Depending on the feature you choose, this can include injury/recovery notes,
physical limitations, body measurements, effort and exercise records, related
progress calculations, or photos/videos that reveal a condition or physical
state. Combined training fields can reveal health even when an individual field
looks ordinary. Device/provider data can include additional sensitive fields
only if that genuine integration is enabled and separately authorized.

The reviewed web logger records exercises, repetitions, load, distance, duration,
session effort and optional notes/media. That does not prove that heart-rate,
sleep, menstrual, medical-record or continuous-location imports are operational
or authorized. Do not enter someone else's health information or medical records
into an ordinary workout/support field.

## 3. Defined Purposes And Legal Basis

The proposed health-feature purposes are to retain your requested private
training record, display its history and calculate the progress information
requested by that feature. Optional coach disclosure, provider import and public
sharing are distinct decisions, not automatically part of private logging.

Where explicit consent is the applicable condition, KRUXT must establish both
an ordinary GDPR legal basis and an Article 9 condition before processing.
For these proposed consent-based purposes, the intended bases are Articles
6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a). Merely accepting a contract, acknowledging a privacy notice
or entering text is not a substitute for the required explicit choice. Any
different basis proposed by a gym or for a narrowly required legal purpose must
be assessed and explained specifically; it is not presumed by this notice.

The operator must not repurpose health data for advertising, sale to data
brokers, insurance/employment eligibility, unrelated sensitive profiling or
general-purpose AI training under this consent. A materially different purpose
requires a separate lawful assessment and, where appropriate, fresh consent.

## 4. Separate Choices - Proposed Wording, Not Recorded Consent

The following controls are **specifications for implementation and review**.
They are not a record that you or any tester has agreed. Nothing in this file
activates an integration or changes a user's existing choices.

### A. Private Health-Related Training Features

Unchecked control, shown before the relevant health processing:

> I explicitly consent to [CONTROLLER NAME], operating KRUXT, storing and using the
> health information I choose to enter in [identified training features] to
> maintain my private training history and calculate the progress described
> above. I understand how to withdraw this consent.

Specify the enabled data fields and purposes beside the choice. It must not
authorize unrelated coaching, marketing, tracking or public disclosure.

### B. Optional Disclosure To A Named Gym Or Coach

Separate unchecked control at the relevant assignment/disclosure:

> I explicitly consent to sharing [identified health fields and purpose] with
> [named gym/coach and relevant authorized role] for [specific coaching purpose].
> This choice is separate from my private training record.

Do not render the bracketed fields as generic "partners" or obtain unlimited
permission for future gyms. Changing the recipient or purpose needs another
appropriate choice. Review how withdrawing affects information already held by
a separate controller and explain that controller's contact and duties.

### C. Optional Provider Connection

Before each genuine import, name the provider, requested data categories,
purposes, direction/frequency of transfer and recipients. Obtain the necessary
in-app choice and platform/provider permissions. A Google/Apple sign-in button
does not authorize Google fitness or Apple Health access. A pending connection
or a consent record alone is not permission to fetch unsupported data.

### D. Optional Sharing Of A Specific Post

Private logging must finish without requiring a post. Before a health-revealing
post is shared, identify the actual item and audience and apply the appropriate
explicit disclosure choice where required. Do not infer this from general
onboarding consent or assume that a public-setting change cures all Article 9
issues. Explain that public recipients may save and redistribute material.
Do not publish another person's health information merely because they appear
in your photo or workout. High-risk sharing should remain unavailable until
the audience, reporting and withdrawal/removal paths are verified.

## 5. Refusal And Withdrawal

Refusing or withdrawing a consent must stop the associated future processing
where consent is required. The affected health function may no longer work,
but unrelated membership/account functions must not be withheld merely to
pressure you into an unnecessary consent. Whether a particular training function
can operate without health processing must be assessed honestly, not assumed.

The proposed route is an equally accessible in-app privacy control and the
privacy contact [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. **Before enabling processing,
verify and publish the actual working route**. Withdrawal must cover future
collection, imports, calculations and disclosures within the consent's scope;
changing a checkbox must not leave background processing running unnoticed.

Withdrawal does not retrospectively invalidate lawful earlier processing. We
must erase or otherwise stop retaining the affected data where there is no
remaining lawful ground, including derived health information. Any exception
needs an identified legal basis, limited scope and retention period. We must
not retain the entire training history indefinitely just to keep proof of consent.

## 6. Who Can See It, Storage And Retention

The intended default is no social publication without a deliberate choice.
That does not mean end-to-end encryption or exclusion of justified, authorized
service support. Staff access must match role, tenant, assignment, purpose and
current authorization. A follower request or gym affiliation is not by itself
permission for private health data.

The Privacy Notice's supplier/transfer schedule must be completed and verified
for health processing before activation. Do not assume all hosting, logs, email or remote support remains
in the EU. A withdrawn integration may leave a provider's separate copy; use its
own deletion/rights route where needed.

## 7. Limits, Rights And Complaints

KRUXT does not provide emergency monitoring or clinical oversight. A coach's
ability to see a note does not mean the note will be reviewed promptly or safely
replace medical care. Progress and challenge scores must not be used as medical
diagnoses or pressure to exercise against your judgment.

Use the privacy contact or working request controls for access, correction,
deletion, restriction or other applicable rights. The Privacy Notice explains
response timing and complaints, including the Italian Garante where appropriate.
No automated medical, insurance or employment decision is authorized here.

## 8. Implementation Blocker

account-wide consent and presents combined acceptance. This draft does not
approve that design. Separate Terms acceptance, Privacy Notice acknowledgment,
and specific health choices; implement server-enforced feature gating and tested
withdrawal before enabling these consent-based features. Do not activate a
policy version or treat this draft's existence as collected explicit consent.
