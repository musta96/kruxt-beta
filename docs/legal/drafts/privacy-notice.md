in the reviewed DEV repair defaults to private; older records keep their previous
settings and each deployed client must be checked. Review the actual audience

authorized service personnel can never access information for a justified purpose.

## 6. Storage On Your Device

The web client uses browser storage for authentication/session continuity and
some task state. A shared device can expose that information to its other users;
sign out and manage browser storage appropriately.

An optional workout recovery draft stores sensitive exercise text and metrics
in the current tab's session storage, not encrypted by KRUXT on the device. It
does not include uploaded files. In the reviewed local implementation it is off
by default, becomes invalid after 12 hours without an update and is removed when
the application next checks an expired draft. This is not guaranteed physical
deletion at the exact deadline; browser session-restore behavior may preserve
storage. Turning the option off attempts to remove its workout content; a
removal failure is surfaced rather than treated as completed deletion. Separate
submission-identity/reconciliation metadata can remain and needs its own retention
rule. Confirm this behavior on each
released device/client before publishing the notice as a live-feature description.

The reviewed local gym administration implementation can retain class-publication
recovery content in tab storage with a 24-hour validity rule. Cleanup while active
or on resume replaces expired content with identifier-only recovery records;
suspended/restored tabs can retain private content beyond 24 hours until checked.
This is not a verified hosted deployment or a guaranteed timed physical erasure.
Selected-gym preferences and some task metadata may persist separately.

## 8. Your Rights And Contact

Subject to the applicable conditions, you can ask for access, correction,
erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing. You can withdraw a
consent for future processing without invalidating earlier lawful processing.
You can object to direct marketing at any time. Contact
[PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] or use the available privacy-request controls; a broken in-app control
does not remove your rights. We may seek proportionate identity confirmation,
not unnecessary documents.

We respond without undue delay and normally within one month. If a legally
permitted extension is needed, we explain the reasons within the initial month.
Rights are generally free; narrowly defined legal exceptions and the rights of
other people may affect how a request is fulfilled. We will explain a refusal or
limitation and your options, rather than silently marking it complete.

You can complain to a competent supervisory authority, including where you live,
work or believe a breach occurred. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione
dei dati personali: [garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/).
Contacting us first is helpful but is not a condition of complaint.
